submit it separately from any Contribution, identifying the complete details of

Telangana shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## Signing

Signature is recorded electronically against Your GitHub account via CLA
Assistant when You open Your first pull request. The record of that signature —
Your GitHub username, the commit, and the timestamp — constitutes Your
acceptance of this Agreement.
